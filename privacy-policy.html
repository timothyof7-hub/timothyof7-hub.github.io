Privacy Policy for Dolphin Pod

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Dolphin Pod ("the app") is a personal relationship check-in app. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it's used, and how advertising works.

No account required

Dolphin Pod does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address to use it.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally, on your device only:

• Names, relationships, group tags, phone numbers, and birthdays for the people you add to your pod
• Check-in dates, notes, and streak counts
• Your notification time and app color theme preferences

This information is not transmitted to any server operated by us. There is no backend for your pod data — everything lives in the app's local storage on your device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Permissions we request

Contacts (optional). If you use the "Import from Contacts" feature, the app reads your device's contact list so you can select people to add to your pod. Contact information is copied into the app's local storage only when you explicitly select a contact — it is never uploaded anywhere, and the app does not access your contacts unless you open this feature.

Notifications. The app schedules local reminder notifications (daily check-in reminders, birthday reminders, and occasional "thinking of you" nudges) directly on your device. These are not sent from a server — they're generated and delivered entirely on-device.

Advertising

Dolphin Pod uses Google AdMob to display advertising. To do this, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

• Device identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID)
• IP address and general location
• Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is used to serve and measure advertising and is subject to Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

EU/UK consent (GDPR / UK-GDPR). If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, the app will show a consent prompt — powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) — before any personalized advertising is served, in accordance with Google's EU User Consent Policy. You can choose to decline personalized ads; you will still see advertising, but it will not be personalized to you.

Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS). On iOS 14.5 and later, if ad personalization would use your device's advertising identifier, the app will show Apple's own tracking permission prompt ("Ask App Not to Track" / "Allow") before doing so. You can decline this while still using the app normally; declining limits ad personalization but does not affect any app functionality.

Children's privacy

Dolphin Pod is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you control it directly: deleting a person removes their data from the app, and uninstalling the app removes all app data. There is no account to delete because none is created.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if advertising, analytics, or a backend is added in the future — this document will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
