Frequently asked questions

How do I add someone to my pod?

Tap the + button on the Main screen to add someone manually, or use "Import from Contacts" to pull people in from your device's contact list.

How often will I be reminded to check in with someone?

You set a custom check-in cadence for each person individually — anywhere from every day to every 150 days. Dolphin Pod sends a daily reminder if anyone's overdue, based on your own preferred reminder time in Settings.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. Dolphin Pod has no account system and no backend server for your pod data — nothing about the people you track is ever uploaded anywhere.

How do I unlock extra themes, Insights, or more pod slots?

Watch a couple of short ads. Every locked feature can be unlocked that way, and unlocks are kept on your device — there is nothing to buy.

I found a bug or have a feature request — what do I do?

Email us at the address above with as much detail as you can (what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead). We read every message.
